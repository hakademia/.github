# Hakademia Code of Conduct

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Organization:** Hakademia  
**Contact:** conduct@hakademia.dev  
**Applies to:** All members, maintainers, contributors, collaborators, and community participants.

## 1. Our Commitment

Hakademia is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, respectful, and collaborative environment where developers can learn, build, compete, and create.

We believe that everyone deserves an opportunity to contribute, regardless of their experience, background, identity, nationality, gender, religion, or technical ability.

Our community is built on three principles:

- **Respect people.** Treat every individual with dignity and professionalism.
- **Recognize contributions.** Give appropriate credit to creators, maintainers, and contributors.
- **Build together.** Encourage collaboration, knowledge sharing, and constructive competition.

This Code of Conduct applies to all official Hakademia projects, repositories, events, communication channels, and community activities.

## 2. Expected Behavior

All participants are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and professionally.
- Welcome newcomers and support less experienced developers.
- Provide constructive feedback during discussions and code reviews.
- Respect different opinions, technical approaches, and cultural backgrounds.
- Recognize original creators and contributors.
- Respect repository permissions, licenses, and intellectual property rights.
- Protect confidential information and private repositories.
- Follow the rules of coding competitions and hackathons.
- Report serious misconduct or security concerns through appropriate channels.
- Represent Hakademia responsibly when participating in official activities.

## 3. Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are prohibited:

- Harassment, discrimination, bullying, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Hate speech, threats, or abusive language.
- Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Plagiarism, code theft, or deliberately misrepresenting authorship.
- Unauthorized access to private repositories, infrastructure, or accounts.
- Sharing confidential code, credentials, tokens, or internal information without authorization.
- Deliberately damaging repositories, systems, or development workflows.
- Cheating, manipulating results, or violating competition rules.
- Abusing organizational privileges or resources.
- Retaliating against individuals who report misconduct in good faith.

These rules apply regardless of a participant's position or role within Hakademia.

## 4. Organization Membership and Responsibilities

Hakademia operates through three membership circles and external collaboration.

### Core Team

Members responsible for developing and maintaining Hakademia's core platform, architecture, security, and infrastructure.

Core members must protect confidential information, follow security policies, and exercise their privileges responsibly.

### Ecosystem Team

Members developing plugins, SDKs, integrations, documentation, and supporting tools.

Ecosystem members must respect the separation between ecosystem projects and private core repositories.

### Community Team

Members creating public projects, participating in hackathons, and contributing to open-source initiatives.

Community members must follow project documentation standards, respect other contributors, and acknowledge original creators.

### External Contributors

External contributors may participate in authorized projects without becoming full organization members.

They are expected to follow this Code of Conduct when participating in Hakademia activities.

Membership in any circle does not exempt anyone from these rules.

## 5. Project Ownership and Creator Recognition

Hakademia recognizes the importance of individual creativity and contribution.

Every project hosted within the organization should clearly identify:

- **Original Creator:** The person or people who originally conceived and initiated the project.
- **Maintainers:** Individuals responsible for ongoing development and maintenance.
- **Contributors:** Individuals who have contributed code, documentation, designs, or other meaningful work.

Project repositories should maintain accurate attribution through documentation and contribution history.

Hosting a repository within Hakademia does not, by itself, transfer copyright or intellectual property rights.

Copyright, licensing, and ownership are governed by applicable agreements, licenses, and Hakademia's governance policies.

## 6. Collaboration and Code Reviews

Hakademia encourages open technical discussions and constructive collaboration.

Participants should:

- Discuss technical ideas without attacking individuals.
- Provide clear and respectful feedback.
- Explain major architectural decisions when appropriate.
- Respect established review and approval processes.
- Avoid unnecessary conflicts and disruptive behavior.
- Resolve disagreements through discussion or maintainer review.

Maintainers are expected to apply contribution rules fairly and consistently.

## 7. Competitions and Hackathons

Hakademia supports competitive programming, developer challenges, and hackathons.

All participants must:

- Follow the official rules of each competition.
- Respect other participants and organizers.
- Submit original work or properly attribute permitted external resources.
- Avoid unauthorized collaboration, cheating, and manipulation.
- Respect competition deadlines and evaluation procedures.

Organizers must act fairly, disclose relevant conflicts of interest, and apply published rules consistently.

## 8. Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, you may report it privately.

**Official reporting contact: conduct@hakademia.dev**

Reports should include, when possible:

- A description of the incident.
- The date and approximate time.
- The repository, event, or communication channel involved.
- Relevant screenshots, messages, or other supporting evidence.
- Any immediate safety concerns.

Anonymous reports may be considered, although limited information can make investigations more difficult.

Do not publish sensitive reports in public GitHub Issues, Discussions, or Pull Requests.

### Confidentiality

Hakademia will handle reports discreetly and share information only with individuals who reasonably need it to review or resolve the incident.

Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed, particularly where disclosure is necessary for safety, fair review, or legal compliance.

### Conflicts of Interest

If a report concerns a person responsible for handling complaints, that person should not participate in reviewing or deciding the case.

Where possible, the report should be assigned to another impartial administrator.

## 9. Enforcement Guidelines

Hakademia administrators may take appropriate action when violations occur.

Possible actions include:

**Level 1 — Guidance**

Private communication explaining the concern and expected behavior.

**Level 2 — Formal Warning**

A documented warning describing the violation and required corrective actions.

**Level 3 — Temporary Restrictions**

Temporary suspension of repository permissions, team access, or participation in community activities.

**Level 4 — Removal**

Removal from a project, Team, event, or organization.

**Level 5 — Permanent Ban**

Permanent exclusion from Hakademia activities for severe or repeated misconduct.

Enforcement decisions should consider the severity, context, evidence, and risk of further harm.

Serious incidents may require immediate protective measures without following every preceding level.

## 10. Fair Review and Appeals

Hakademia aims to handle reports fairly and consistently.

When appropriate and safe, affected individuals should have an opportunity to provide their perspective.

A person subject to an enforcement decision may request reconsideration by contacting:

**conduct@hakademia.dev**

Where possible, appeals should be reviewed by someone who was not responsible for the original decision.

Submitting an appeal does not automatically suspend an enforcement action.

## 11. Security and Confidentiality

All members must protect Hakademia's systems and information.

Participants must not:

- Disclose private repository contents without authorization.
- Share credentials, access tokens, or sensitive configuration.
- Attempt to bypass repository permissions.
- Use organizational resources for unauthorized activities.
- Exploit discovered vulnerabilities.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported through the organization's designated security reporting process rather than public discussions.

## 12. Scope and Representation

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- Official Hakademia GitHub repositories.
- Organization-managed communication channels.
- Official coding competitions and hackathons.
- Community events and collaborative projects.
- Other activities where individuals are formally representing Hakademia.

Personal opinions expressed by members do not automatically represent the official position of Hakademia.

Members must not claim to speak on behalf of the organization without authorization.

## 13. Updates and Amendments

Hakademia may revise this Code of Conduct as the organization evolves.

Material changes should be documented and communicated through appropriate organizational channels.

Changes become effective once approved and published by authorized organization administrators.
